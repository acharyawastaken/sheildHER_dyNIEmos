# AI Rules & Constraints

## Privacy Rules
- Do not store raw audio/video permanently
- Collect only minimal required data
- Prefer on-device processing

## Safety Rules
- Avoid false aggressive alerts
- Always allow manual override
- Do not trigger SOS blindly without context

## AI Design Rules
- Risk scoring must be explainable
- Avoid black-box decision systems
- Use threshold-based logic

## Ethical Rules
- No selling user data
- No ads during emergencies
- Full transparency with users
